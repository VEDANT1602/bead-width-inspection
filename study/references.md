# Reference and result register

1. Badarinath & Prabhu (2022), [primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8812280/), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma15020618). Inspected sections 4.2 and 4.3, Figure 10, the width-error description of Appendix A.2 and the license on page 1 of the full PDF. Section 4.3 reports a maximum MAE of 0.0325 mm and RMSE of 0.0371 mm over materials/colors. The latter is not a measured camera SD. Figure 10 shows smoothed widths and periodic caliper points for five deposition speeds. The source reports 50 raw caliper readings per path and marks 11 positions on the plot. No claim is made that these are 11 independent raw observations. Publisher PDF SHA-256: 00418e59b0c2159e84c159984818d80b8128ade7bc06232ea9eab1af15c8992d.
2. NIST/SEMATECH, [EWMA Control Charts, section 6.3.1.4](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section3/pmc314.htm). Definition, weighting parameter and control-limit calculation support the named EWMA comparator. Correlated observations and mandatory checks change its ordinary run-length interpretation.
3. NIST/SEMATECH, [Cusum Control Charts, section 6.3.1.3](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section3/pmc313.htm). Tabular allowance and threshold support the two-sided comparator. The observation and reset rules in this note are specified in reference/compare.py.
4. Kalman (1960), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552), [full publication copy](https://cs-www.cs.yale.edu/homes/yry/readings/general/Kalman1960.pdf). “Solution of the Wiener Problem” and subsequent recursion provide state/covariance updating. The local discrepancy model, the prior in the tolerance benchmark and our variance estimates are specific to the present analyses.

## Resolving the two values displayed as 9.35

[Published simulation and table, immutable commit](https://github.com/VEDANT1602/bead-width-inspection/tree/7d9d76dcde1ce0291224bcf7cf6a64756a2ef5d3). The nominal periodic-reset average was 9.3502604167, and the periodic Kalman average at assigned SD 0.0371 mm was 9.35234375. Their integer event totals differ by eight. Each average uses 3,840 builds. scripts/verify.py reconstructs both results by applying independent vector-state/matrix-covariance and scalar-smoothing formulas to the underlying simulation trajectories. That verification is independent of the comparator implementation, not of the generated trajectories and fault assumptions.

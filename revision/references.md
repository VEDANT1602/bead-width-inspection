## Search for usable field traces

The Cambridge Caxton dataset’s official description enumerates each image’s labels: flow and motion settings, temperature, time, and position. The listed labels do not include an independent physical width measured at each candidate segment. [Cambridge repository](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/6d77cd6d-8569-4bf4-9d5f-311ad2a49ac8)

Shabani and Martinez Hernandez report an in-situ camera system and accuracy for submillimetre extrusion. The accessible article record contains aggregate performance. It does not furnish a paired sequence for replay. [University of Bath record, abstract and document access](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/in-situ-measurement-of-extrusion-width-for-fused-filament-fabrica-2/)

A Dryad dataset records process sensor readings and cylinder-level pressurization outcomes. Those are different labels and observation units. [Dryad study data and description](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.stqjq2c9v)

Liu et al. (2019), cited in the previous version, supports the broader claim on image-based process control in its publisher abstract and preview. The full paper was not retrieved. It has been replaced in this manuscript by [1], which addresses the independent width measurement directly and provides a checkable physical error scale. [Liu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmsy.2019.04.002)

No papers identified in this targeted search supplied a directly reusable, every-candidate aligned camera/reference series with independently controlled process shifts and optical shifts. This is the result of the search, not a claim that no such dataset exists.
